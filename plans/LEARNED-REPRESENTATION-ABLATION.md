# The Learned Sparse Representation: A Lab Plan

## 1. What the previous run established, and what it cannot

The previous run established exactly one thing. On mean-pooled esm2_150m vectors, a single affine map from 640 dimensions to 2048, fit by mean squared error of the code cosine onto the information-content-weighted Lin similarity of two proteins' propagated GO closures, raises the held-out Spearman correlation against that same Lin target from +0.168 to +0.530, and keeping the 128 largest-magnitude coordinates gives back 0.063 of that to land at +0.467. Measured on 4,000 proteins absent from the training pool, over 120,000 pairs. That is the whole of the result, and every sentence that has been built on top of it exceeds it.

It cannot establish anything about anisotropy, because nothing in `encoder_ablation.py` measures anisotropy. There is no mean pairwise cosine, no participation ratio, no spectral statistic, no isotropy score of any kind. The geometric claim was assumed by the shape of the arm list and never tested. Worse, the one unsupervised comparator, `pca256`, is built from sklearn's `PCA`, which centres by construction, and the learned arm is `nn.Linear(d, arm.dict_dim)` at line 221 with the default `bias=True`, which can also centre. Mean subtraction, the textbook anisotropy repair, is therefore silently inside every arm except `dense`, unseparated from whatever else that arm is doing. No dense-to-learned difference in that run is attributable to geometry.

It cannot establish that sparsification costs 0.063. The loss at lines 225 to 235 computes the cosine on the full dense projection `z = enc(Rt)`; `topk_real` appears only in `apply_encoder` at line 248. The encoder is never trained through the selection. The 0.063 is the price of a train-and-apply mismatch, and it has never been a measurement of sparsity. Three of the four candidate designs converged on this independently and it is the correction with the largest downstream consequence in this document.

It cannot establish a retrieval result, because Spearman against Lin is the training objective. The encoder minimises squared error of code cosine against Lin and is then scored by rank correlation against Lin. That is held-out training loss, and only one arm family was ever given the target. `dense` and `pca256` were not. Separately, those three numbers do not come from `encoder_ablation.py` at all: that module computes no correlation and its only readout is `f_micro_w`. They come from `scripts/run_sdr_c_contrastive.py`, a different harness with a different split. The method card and the ablation module are two experiments wearing one name.

It cannot establish generalisation. In `run_encoder_ablation` the encoder is fit on the reference pool and then scored by retrieving from that same pool: `closures` and `ref_closures` are the same object. The fit is 1.31 million parameters against 60,000 references, 150 full-batch epochs of Adam at 1e-3, with no weight decay, no dropout, no validation split and no early stopping. On top of that, `_load_data` excludes queries by accession while embeddings key on `sequence_id`. With 575,503 bank accessions over 487,237 distinct sequences, an identical-vector donor survives the exclusion at cosine exactly 1.0 and is a guaranteed top-1. The size of that exposure is a range and not a count, which an earlier draft of this document got wrong by reporting 88,266 accessions, 15.3 percent, as though it were the number sharing a vector. 88,266 is the EXCESS of accessions over distinct sequences. If every shared sequence were held by exactly two accessions the exposed set is 176,532, which is 30.7 percent; if the excess were concentrated in one enormous group it is 88,267, which is 15.3 percent. So 15.3 percent is the floor of the exposure and 30.7 percent its ceiling, and the true figure is one group-size histogram away and should be measured before the gate is set.

It cannot be reproduced. The reference draw at line 391 is `SELECT DISTINCT` with a hash modulo and no `ORDER BY`, so `rng.shuffle` with seed 42 permutes the positions of a differently ordered list and selects a different 60,000 proteins on every run. `torch.manual_seed` is present in seven ancestor scripts and absent from the module, so the two learned arms differ in initialisation as well as in objective. Information content is estimated from the drawn pool at line 203, so the regression targets themselves move when the pool moves and no two runs sit on a common scale.

It cannot be defended on substrate. It ran on the smallest model in the grid, chosen because it was smallest, while prot_t5 at 1024 dimensions and ankh_large at 1536 reach 0.3414 and 0.3346 at K=3 under the embedding-isolating preset. And it was scored at K=30, which the K ablation has since shown to be the worst available setting: that axis closed at 24 of 24, K=3 wins in seven of eight models, and the decay is monotone in seven of eight. K is not free to change in the present code, because hard-negative mining reads `spec.knn` at line 213, so moving the evaluation K moves the training set.

Sparsity was sampled at one point, and it is the least informative point available. The collision number, the expected count of dictionary atoms two independent k-sparse codes share by chance, is k squared over D. At 128 and 2048 that is 8, and the fraction of pairs sharing at least one atom is 1 minus exp(-8), which is 0.99966. Every pair in the bank collides. That is a statement about an inverted index rather than about geometry, since two codes sharing 8 atoms out of 128 still have 94 percent of each support unshared, but it is enough to say that the door the author believes sparsity opens was measured at the one corner where the door is shut.

Finally, in its present state on this machine the module reports nothing at all. `gt_dir` at line 88 and the default Python path both point at `/home/frapercan`, missing since the reinstall, so the cafaeval subprocess fails into an error dictionary, every cell metric stays `None`, and `run.json` is still written with status ok.

## 2. Substrate, scoring, and the confound that governs the plan

The confound is that a learned code on a weak substrate may recover only what a stronger substrate already had. This is not a hypothetical. The project has already measured it once: the identical `_train_encoder` recipe at hard-neg, identical 100k pool, identical 15k scoring reference, identical 2048 by 128 at seed 42, reaches mean-nine `f_micro_w` of 0.22013 on the production ankh_base embedding and 0.1447 on a local layer-48 mean pool of the same model. The base is worth 0.075. The clean learned-head benefit on a shared base was 0.008. The substrate lever is nine times the head lever, already, on the record. Any design that varies the head on one substrate is measuring the small thing with the large thing nailed down.

So substrate is an axis, and it carries the primary inference. Three rungs, with a fourth held in reserve.

| rung | dimension | role |
|---|---|---|
| esm2_150m | 640 | bridge to the existing number, weakest substrate, the end with the most headroom |
| esm2_650m | 1280 | same family, same tokenizer, same corpus, 4.3 times the parameters |
| prot_t5 | 1024 | different family, the K=3 leader at 0.3414, nearly the same width as esm2_650m |

The 150m to 650m step holds family fixed and varies capacity. The 650m to prot_t5 step crosses family at almost equal width, so a difference there is quality rather than dimension. ankh_large enters only if the encoder-by-substrate interaction lands inside its interval, and that decision is made from the measured interval, not by eye. Design four proposed six rungs including esm2_3b, prostt5 and esmc_600m. It is cut to three because the marginal rung buys no new contrast, enlarges the Holm family for nothing, and because the premise that made six look free, that all configs are already in the cache, is asserted nowhere gated. One missing config costs between 9.63 and 24.1 GPU hours, derived below, which is more than the whole fitting budget of this plan.

The pre-registered interaction contrast is written before any fit: the encoder gain at prot_t5 minus the encoder gain at esm2_150m, paired per protein. If it is strongly negative, the encoder substitutes for backbone capacity and the correct thesis sentence is that a small model can be made to behave like a larger one, which is a product claim and not a representation claim. If it is zero or positive, the gain is independent of substrate quality and the representation claim survives that particular attack.

Scoring is fixed as follows. K equals 3, because the K ablation closed at 24 of 24 and K=30 dilutes precisely the neighbourhood a sharper geometry is supposed to sharpen. A fan over K in 1, 3, 10 and 30 runs on the two bracketing substrates only, and it is nearly free because the top-200 neighbour lists are computed once and the lower values of K are prefixes of them. Mining is decoupled from evaluation in code, with `mine_k` frozen at 30 so that the training set is identical across every scoring K. This spends the mining-radius axis to buy independence between K and the encoder, and it is worth it, because at present those two are welded together by a single field.

Vote weighting is a scoring condition, not a detail. `knn_transfer` at line 292 weights every donor by its raw cosine and only zeroes the negatives. Under anisotropy the three donors of a K=3 vote score something like 0.970, 0.965 and 0.960, which is an almost uniform three-way vote; after centring or whitening the same three donors might score 0.42, 0.28 and 0.19, which is nearly a pure top-1 vote. Identical ranking, identical retrieved set, materially different prediction. So every arm is scored twice, once with cosine weights and once with uniform weights over the top K, and the difference between those two conditions is a reported quantity. This is what makes the author's own framing measurable: if a repair only changes the weights, the ranking really was absorbing the defect and the repair bought a rescaling. Relatedly, the platform's `vote_fraction` preset must not be used for this question. Its vote increment is an information-accretion ratio divided by K with the embedding similarity weight at zero, so the cosine is multiplied by zero and any arm that leaves the retrieved set unchanged scores bit-identically to `dense`. Scoring the anisotropy question through it would guarantee that the refutation fires for reasons of instrumentation.

The reference bank is the full annotated set at the cut, not 11.4 percent of it. The present draw cannot produce that: the modulo divisor is `max(2, 556000 // (ref_n * 2))`, which at a requested 520,000 evaluates to `max(2, 0)`, capping the candidate pool near half the corpus. The draw is replaced with an ordered explicit selection.

Two separations carry the design. First, the fit pool is made disjoint from the index pool. Fit on hash bucket A, retrieve exclusively from disjoint bucket B, and report the shipped condition of 100 percent overlap beside the 0 percent condition. Second, the query exclusion moves to `sequence_id` and is gated to a count of zero before any run starts, because moving from K=30 to K=3 multiplies each leaked donor's share of the vote roughly tenfold. Homology closure uses MMseqs2 at 30 percent identity and 0.8 coverage over all 487,237 sequences, and it must land as a registered operation, because mmseqs, cd-hit and foldseek are all absent from this machine and the homology band the campaign mandates currently has no producer at all. The closed pool is paired with a matched control: an equal number of query-free clusters removed, matched on cluster size, annotation count, mean best-IC and length band. Design three proposed reporting open minus closed as the leakage estimate. That was refuted: the clusters removed by closure are the reviewed spine, the most deeply and specifically annotated proteins in the corpus, so open minus closed is a composition shift, and only closed against closed-matched isolates leakage. Open minus closed is retained as an upper bound and labelled as one.

## 3. The axes and their levels

**Layers.** Not a primary axis in this plan, and the reason is a refutation rather than a budget. Design one made fractional depth primary on the hypothesis that depth and anisotropy are one axis. The ledger's own numbers refute the mechanism: centring takes the mean random-pair cosine from 0.858 to 0.013 on esm2_650m and from 0.594 to minus 0.001 on ankh_base, and after that complete repair 80.5 percent and 86.5 percent of nearest neighbours are unchanged and 63.4 percent of the top thirty survive. A full anisotropy repair is a modest reordering. The prior destroyed ablation had already concluded that the raw layer choice is statistically null and standardisation is the lever, and the effect on record was 0.012 against a floor that is at least 0.0082 in the frame where the headline lives. The layer axis is not resolvable where it matters, and its full form cost 52.6 GPU hours of dump plus 44 uncosted hours of confirmation. What survives is one observation worth keeping: `output_hidden_states=True` is unconditional at both load and call in all three backends, so the entire hidden-state tuple is already inside the measured GPU peaks and pooling every layer costs no extra memory and under one percent of extra wall time. The standing rule that follows is that any forward pass this project pays for any reason pools and stores all layers. Inside this plan, layers appear as a single three-level probe at fractional depth 1.00, 0.75 and 0.50 on esm2_150m over a length-stratified 60,000-sequence sample, run to decide whether the isotropy profile has structure across depth at all, and reported as descriptive.

**Sparsity.** A two-parameter surface, plotted against the collision number and against measured support overlap, never against nominal collision alone. Dictionary width is expressed as overcompleteness rather than as an absolute count, because pinning D at 2048 while d runs from 640 to 2560 makes the ratio D over d fall monotonically from 3.20 to 0.80 along the primary axis, and the pre-registered signature of the recovery reading is exactly what that confound predicts. Design four pinned it in absolute terms so that the substrate contrast would be readable; the refutation is that the pinning welds a capacity gradient to the axis under test.

| substrate | dictionary levels | active atoms |
|---|---|---|
| esm2_150m (d=640) | 640, 2048, 5120, 10240 | 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, dense |
| esm2_650m (d=1280) | 1280, 4096, 10240, 20480 | cross only: all k at 4096, k=128 at every D |
| prot_t5 (d=1024) | 1024, 3277, 8192, 16384 | cross only: all k at 3277, k=128 at every D |

2048 is retained on esm2_150m purely as the bridge to the shipped point. The collision numbers spanned run from 8 squared over 10240, which is 0.00625, to 256 squared over 640, which is 102, four decades. The pre-registered anchors sit at collision 8 (the shipped corner, candidate fraction 0.99966), 1.0 (0.632), 0.25 (0.221) and 0.031 (0.031). Because the map is affine and atoms with large bias are selected for every protein, the uniform-support assumption behind those candidate fractions fails at the architecture level, so effective width is reported as the exponential of the atom-usage entropy, the effective collision number is recomputed from it, and the random-projection control is matched on measured candidate fraction rather than on the nominal value.

The dense readout at every dictionary width is a free convergence check, and it becomes a valid one only with the bias removed. For a linear map under a cosine loss the similarity depends on W only through W transpose W, a symmetric 640 by 640 form, which after removing the scale invariance is 205,119 free parameters regardless of dictionary width, so the dense arm must be flat in D or the fit did not converge. Design two derived this and then reasoned as if the map were linear; it is affine, `nn.Linear` carries a bias by default, and the arithmetic gives it away, since the 1,312,768 being rejected is 640 times 2048 plus 2048. Cosine of two affine images does not factor through W transpose W. The plan therefore sets `bias=False` as the default and keeps `bias=True` as one named control arm, because with the bias present the centring control is nested inside the treatment and the anisotropy comparison collapses.

**Training protocol.** The effective sample size is proteins, not pairs, because pairs sharing an endpoint are not independent observations of a metric-learning problem. So the ladder is pool size in proteins at 15,000, 60,000 and 120,000 with the pair budget fixed at 2,000,000, plus one legacy rung at 60,000 proteins and 300,000 pairs. The pair sampler has two levels, uniform as shipped and length-mass allocated at 0.5322, 0.4311 and 0.0368 across the bands at or below 512, 513 to 2048, and above 2048, with a per-taxon anchor cap of 2 percent. The mass-allocated sampler is a treatment and not a fix, because the evaluation metric is IA-mass weighted and reported by length band, so a long-protein gain under a sampler aligned to that band is attributable to the sampler; the uniform arm at the anchor is what prices it.

Optimisation has two levels. The legacy level is 150 full-batch steps, which is what `epochs=150` actually means, since the loop performs one backward and one step per epoch. That bounds each weight's total displacement at 150 times 1e-3, which is 0.15, against an initialisation scale of 1 over the square root of 640, which is 0.0395, so the fit is not obviously unconverged; but nothing has ever demonstrated that it converged, because the loss is written to the Python logger every 30 epochs and to nothing else and there is no validation split. The working level moves `opt.step()` inside the pair-batch loop and runs 3,000 minibatch steps at pair batch 16,384 with cosine decay from 1e-3 to 1e-5, which is 24.6 passes over 2,000,000 pairs and 20 times the gradient evaluations of the legacy setting at a fraction of its cost. Both levels log a validation loss curve on a held-out cluster split, and a run without a curve is not a result. Batch size is bounded by `pair_bs = min(16384, floor(1.4e8 / D))` so that the gathered code tensors stay near a gigabyte and the peak stays near 5 GB of the 11,643 MiB free.

Seeds are three separate streams: pool, pairs and initialisation. Within a comparison the pool and pair seeds are fixed so arms are paired, and only initialisation varies, at five replicates. Five is where the t multiplier stops dominating, since it runs 12.71, 4.30, 3.18, 2.78, 2.57 for two through six replicates, giving a per-arm half-width of 1.24 standard deviations at five. Any arm pair whose measured paired-difference standard deviation implies a half-width above 0.01 escalates to twelve replicates, which tightens to 0.635 standard deviations, a factor of 1.95, and a pair that still fails at twelve is reported as unresolved rather than as a result.

**Mechanism.** Four levels, at the three sparsity anchors. M1 is post-hoc top-k by absolute value, the shipped arm. M2 is the same selection inside the loss with a straight-through estimator and a soft-selection temperature annealed from 1.0 to 0.05 over the first tenth of training. M3 is rectification followed by top-k, trained through, which makes support intersection a shared-feature statement and prevents cosines from cancelling; this matters because 49.56 percent of the retained values in the shipped bank are negative while the transfer filters donors on similarity above zero, so nearly half the retained mass sits in a sign the vote discards. M4 is rectification plus a Hoyer penalty bisected to match M3's mean nonzero count. The penalty is Hoyer and not L1, and the derivation is that the loss is a normalised cosine and therefore scale invariant, so an L1 penalty on the code is minimised by shrinking W toward zero without changing the objective at all, whereas Hoyer, the square root of D minus the L1 over L2 ratio, all over the square root of D minus one, is scale free. M2 minus M1 is the single number that says whether the sparsity surface measured sparsity or measured a bug, and three fits of it run before the surface is committed.

**Objective.** This is where the plan earns its keep, because the target has a cheap solution that no previous arm could detect. Lin is twice the information content of the most informative common ancestor divided by the sum of the two proteins' best-IC values, and best-IC is a per-protein scalar, so a large share of the target's variance is explained by two per-protein numbers and no pair information whatsoever. Six levels. O1 is the shipped IC-weighted Lin over the full closure. O2 restricts the target to BPO, because `_max_common_ancestor_ic` applies no aspect filter and MFO terms carry higher information content, so the supervision is probably MFO-dominated while the frontier the project cares about is BPO; a pre-flight measurement reports the aspect of the argmax common ancestor for every training pair. O3 is IC-agnostic Jaccard of the propagated closures, which prices metric alignment, since cafaeval's IA weighting and Lin's information content come from the same frequency table and only the learned arm was told the metric's weights. O4 is the marginal null, target set to twice the pool-mean common-ancestor IC over the same denominator, which deletes all pair information and preserves both per-protein marginals. O5 is the shuffled target, the same values permuted across pairs, which preserves the marginal distribution of the target and destroys everything else. O6 is the label-free cross-backbone target, the raw cosine of a different backbone's embedding, run in both directions.

The decision rules are asymmetric and written now. If O4 recovers half or more of O1's gain over raw, the headline is an annotation-mass prior and not a functional representation. If O5 beats raw at all, every gain number in the campaign is reduced by that offset before any claim is made. And O6 is read on the asymmetry, never on the level: prot_t5 is a measurably better retriever than esm2_150m, so distilling prot_t5 into esm2_150m is substrate transfer wearing a label-free costume. Design two and design three both proposed reading a high cross-backbone score as evidence that a self-supervised programme is worth funding. That is refuted: the confirming outcome is comparable gain in both directions, and gain that runs only downhill in teacher quality is a measurement of teacher quality.

Alongside the trained arms sit the zero-supervision controls, which cost minutes and which the previous run entirely lacked: `dense`, `centered`, `zscore` on bank statistics, `whiten` by Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage ZCA truncated to the rank holding 99 percent of variance with the shrinkage coefficient and the rank reported, `pca256` for continuity, and `random-sparse`, an untrained Gaussian projection through the same selection at matched measured candidate fraction. Whitening without shrinkage is not admissible here: with a participation ratio predicted below 0.10 the covariance is near singular and naive ZCA amplifies noise directions, which would rig the comparison in favour of the learned arm.

## 4. The measurement that makes the anisotropy claim falsifiable

The first half of the claim, that mean-pooled vectors sit in a narrow cone, is already confirmed and quantified and needs no new run beyond repeating the same six statistics on the three substrates of this plan: mean random-pair cosine 0.858 on esm2_650m and 0.594 on ankh_base, norm of the mean direction 0.927 and 0.773, mean cosine after centring 0.013 and minus 0.001. The cone exists and it is almost entirely a translation.

The second half is what needs a test, and the test cannot be mean cosine. Mean cosine is a location statistic: adding a shared direction shifts every pair's cosine by close to a constant, and what survives into a ranking is the per-protein cross term between the mean direction and the residual, which is a hubness effect. A rank metric is nearly blind to the quantity that statistic measures. Worse, a top-k code's mean cosine collapses toward zero by construction as the collision number falls, so reporting it would confirm the thesis sentence vacuously. Everything below is either invariant to sparsification or explicitly null-corrected.

Four measurements, with thresholds fixed before any arm is fit.

The first is the mechanism test, and it can kill the geometric half of the claim on its own. Whitening removes the cone exactly and without supervision. Compute, on the query set against the full bank, the paired per-protein IA-weighted Fmax for `dense` and for `whiten`, together with the isotropy battery for both.

This test is void until the sequence-level exclusion is in place, and the reason is sharper than a general appeal to leakage. An identical input vector maps to an identical output under any deterministic transform, so a twin donor scores cosine exactly 1.0 under dense, under centring, under whitening, under z-scoring and under every code in the grid. The twin is rank 1 in every arm, invariant to every repair. It therefore cannot be cancelled by pairing, and the two-by-two swap of neighbour set against score cannot see it, because the twin sits in both neighbour sets and the swap is designed to separate a better set from better weights.

Worse, it biases the comparison in the direction of confirming the thesis. Under anisotropy the dense arm spreads a K=3 vote almost uniformly, so the guaranteed-correct twin carries about a third of it. After a geometric repair the same three donors separate, the top one takes the largest share, and the top one is the twin. So the repair is credited for concentrating the vote onto a leaked donor that was always going to be right, and the mechanism producing the apparent gain is the same one the vote-weight decomposition was written to expose. Fix the exclusion first, gate it to a count of zero, and only then read this table.

| quantity | confirms the claim | refutes it |
|---|---|---|
| `whiten` isotropy | mean cosine within two null standard deviations of 1 over the square root of d, participation ratio over d above 0.5 | fails to reach isotropy, in which case the arm is broken and the test is void |
| `whiten` minus `dense`, paired, nine cells | exceeds the measured floor in five cells or more | lies inside the floor, and the named defect is demonstrably not what the ranking cares about |
| hubness, skew of the 30-occurrence distribution | raw above 3.0 and repaired below 1.5 | raw below 1.5, so there is no hub pathology to fix |
| separation ratio, top Lin decile minus bottom decile over the random-pair cosine standard deviation | code above dense-supervised at the same capacity, interval excluding parity | parity |

The separation ratio is stated against the dense supervised readout and not against whitening. Design two pre-registered it against whitening at a 1.30 ratio. That is refuted: the statistic is computed over Lin deciles and the code is fit by regression onto Lin, so the test asks whether a Lin regressor separates Lin deciles better than a covariance transform that has never seen Lin, which cannot fail, and its failure branch was the design's stated refutation of the whole geometric claim. Whitening remains in the table as a reference point.

The second is the calibration gap, which is the author's sharper question made numerical. The defect the ranking absorbs is absolute meaning, and absolute meaning is spent at the single global threshold. Define the gap on cafaeval's protein-centric F, as the mean over queries of the maximum over thresholds of that query's F minus its F at the single best global threshold. It is defined on protein-centric F and not on `f_micro_w` because micro pooling makes the per-query oracle non-separable: one query's threshold moves the global precision denominator for every other query, so a per-query argmax is one step of coordinate ascent whose value depends on the order the queries are taken in. Design two defined it on `f_micro_w` over a donor threshold; that is refuted twice over, since micro pooling breaks the oracle and there is no donor threshold in `knn_transfer` at all, which takes K donors by `argpartition` and zeroes only the negatives. The corrected form is a term-score threshold on the frozen prediction matrix, which really is computable on CPU from artifacts the run already writes. Confirmation is a gap of 0.06 or more on `dense` and a gap on the code at or below 0.6 of it, with the interval excluding parity. Refutation is parity, and the honest sentence that follows is that the defect is real and inert and the sparse code does not touch the calibration wall.

The third is the vote decomposition already described, cosine weights against uniform weights. A gain that survives the uniform condition is a better retrieval. A gain that exists only under cosine weights is a rescaling of the vote, which is precisely the ranking absorbing the defect, and it must be reported as such.

The fourth is the two-by-two swap of neighbour set against score, dense set with dense scores, dense set with code scores, code set with dense scores, code set with code scores. Two extra evaluations per substrate and seed, and it separates "found better neighbours" from "weighted the same neighbours better" without any inference.

The floor is measured, never assumed. The declared 0.0034 belongs to a 7,401-protein frame; the headline rests on 1,267 proteins, 399 no-knowledge and 868 limited-knowledge, so naive scaling by the square root of 5.842 puts it at 0.0082 at best. A null-against-null paired bootstrap in this exact frame measures it before any arm is read. And the per-protein delta standard deviation used for the detectable-effect bands is measured from the bridge cell's own output rather than inherited. Design three derived a standard deviation of 0.130 by inverting a Wilcoxon signed-rank z as though it were a t statistic. That is invalid, it understates the standard deviation for a zero-inflated heavy-tailed delta distribution, it narrows the null bands in the direction that manufactures positive findings, and the receipt files it cited no longer exist on disk. Bands are registered from measurement, and any stratum whose minimum detectable effect exceeds the effect under discussion may report a point estimate and may not report a null.

Stratification is computed from a per-protein, per-threshold cache of IA-weighted true positives, false positives and false negatives, extracted once per arm from the single prediction matrix at 7,401 by 100 by 3 by 3 by 4 bytes, which is 26.6 megabytes. Every stratum is then a row-subset sum and every bootstrap resample is a re-indexed sum. This is not an optimisation. Stratifying through cafaeval means one subprocess per stratum, and the mandated grid of nine category-by-aspect cells crossed with four length bands, four homology bands, nine taxa and four propagation bands is 5,184 subprocesses per arm, each paying a fixed 31-megabyte ontology parse of 48,165 terms at roughly 20 seconds, which is 28.8 hours per arm. One full cafaeval run per arm is retained as a cross-check on the nine legacy cells.

Propagation distance is defined arm-independently, from the single cached MMseqs2 pass, as the gap between sequence identity to the nearest bank neighbour of any kind and identity to the nearest bank neighbour carrying experimental evidence at the cut. If the band depended on the arm under test the strata would differ between arms and the pairing would break.

## 5. Cost, derived

A linear map from d to D costs 2dD floating-point operations forward and 4dD backward per row touched, so 6dD in total. A pair touches two rows, so a pass over P pairs costs 12PdD, bounded above, since the unique-row gather touches fewer distinct rows than 2P. At the reference point of d equal to 640, D equal to 2048 and P equal to 2,000,000, that is 12 times 2e6 times 640 times 2048, which is 3.15e13 operations per pass. The card reaches 15 teraflops on this GEMM shape once TF32 is enabled, which is one line that PyTorch has defaulted off since version 1.12 and that the module does not set, so a pass is 2.1 seconds and a 3,000-step fit at pair batch 16,384, which is 24.6 passes, is 52 seconds. Call it one minute at the reference point, and scale linearly in d and in D.

The gate cell, two fit-and-index overlap conditions at three seeds, is six fits and six minutes. The objective battery is six objectives at five seeds on esm2_150m, thirty fits and thirty minutes, plus the reverse cross-backbone direction on prot_t5 at five seeds, which costs 1.6 for the width and 1.6 for the dictionary and so is 2.56 minutes each, 13 minutes, plus five bias-enabled controls at five minutes. The sparsity surface uses one fit per dictionary width serving every k under M1: on esm2_150m the four widths cost 0.3125, 1, 2.5 and 5 minutes, 8.81 per seed and 44 minutes over five seeds; on esm2_650m the width factor is 2 and the four widths sum to 35.25 minutes per seed, 106 minutes over three seeds; on prot_t5 the factor is 1.6 and the sum is 22.6 per seed, 68 minutes over three seeds. That is 218 minutes, 3.6 hours. The mechanisms cost 1.5 times M1 for in-loop selection: two trained-through mechanisms at three anchors whose width factors sum to 8.5, at five seeds, is 128 minutes, and the Hoyer arm with three bisection probes per anchor adds 102 minutes, so 3.8 hours. The protocol ladder is eighteen fits averaging a minute. ## 6. Order of work

The first thing is a coverage query, one count of distinct sequence identifiers per embedding config against the bank's 487,237, run on the server and gated on its exit status. It takes seconds and it decides whether the budget is 13 GPU hours or 37. Nothing else is dispatched until it passes or until the missing extraction is priced into the plan by a 2-percent matched-fraction measurement.

The second is the reporting surface, because the module currently completes, writes status ok, and reports nothing. The two absolute paths under `/home/frapercan` are fixed to the real ground truth at `/home/xaxi/Thesis2/CAFA_forever/data/releases/Sep_2025_Mar_2026/`; a `None` cell metric becomes a hard failure; `spec_hash` gains the harness mode, the terms-of-interest path and the prior-knowledge exclusion path, because the output directory is keyed on that hash and a plain run and an official run of the same spec currently write the same `run.json` and the second destroys the first; and the metrics logger stops returning silently when its tracking URI is unset and stops swallowing every exception.

The third is determinism: an ordered explicit reference draw replacing the hash modulo, `torch.manual_seed` restored from the ancestor script, information content computed once over the full annotation set and written as a hashed artifact, and one frozen pair index with frozen targets so every arm is paired on identical data.

The fourth is the two separations and their producer: sequence-level query exclusion gated to a count of zero, and the MMseqs2 clustering landed as a registered operation, since it does not exist on this machine and the campaign's homology axis depends on it.

The fifth is the optimiser and memory work: the step moved inside the pair batch, TF32 enabled, the unique-row gather adopted from the sibling encoder in the same repository, the apply loop chunked, and the bias removed by default.

The sixth is the bridge cell, the shipped configuration re-run under the frozen protocol at three seeds, from whose per-protein output the delta standard deviation and the null bands are measured and registered.

Then the mechanism gate, which this document originally left out of its own order of work and which the other machine caught. Section 3 states that M2 minus M1 is the single number saying whether the sparsity surface measured sparsity or measured a bug, and that three fits of it run before the surface is committed. Section 6 as first written sequenced the surface and the mechanisms together at the end, so the condition was stated and then not enforced. It is a gate, and it belongs here, because the 3.6-hour sparsity surface is built on one fit per dictionary width serving every k under M1, and M1 is post-hoc truncation of a map fit dense. That is the retracted top-k sweep at scale. If M2 minus M1 is large, every point on that surface prices a train-and-apply mismatch rather than sparsity, and 3.6 GPU hours would buy a mislabelled curve. Three fits, minutes, and they decide whether the surface is worth running at all.

Then, and only then, the two gates that can end the study for the price of an hour. The fit-and-index disjointness condition is six fits and six minutes of GPU, and if the gain collapses to the floor when the encoder cannot retrieve from the proteins it was fit on, the lead lever is a lookup table over the bank and everything downstream is scoring the wrong object. The objective battery is forty fits and under an hour, and if the marginal null recovers half the gain the headline is an annotation-mass prior, while if the shuffled target beats raw at all every number in the campaign carries an offset. Immediately after them come the closed-form controls and the anisotropy battery, which cost minutes and decide whether the geometric framing survives at all.

The sparsity surface, the mechanisms and the substrate ladder run last, because they are the expensive part and because any of the four earlier gates can make them unnecessary. Everything is dispatched as registered operations, `train_functional_encoder`, `apply_functional_encoder`, `score_knn_transfer`, `evaluate_stratified` and the clustering, with a binary `export_embedding_matrix` as the only step that reads a database and with that step on the server's queue, because a procedure outside the platform is a capability that dies with the disk. No single job exceeds thirty minutes, so a reboot of this machine costs one job.

What unblocks the most, stated plainly: the coverage query and the disjointness gate. The first decides the budget, the second decides whether there is a result to spend it on.

## 7. What this plan will not answer

It will not answer the layer question beyond a descriptive three-level probe on one backbone. The geometry regression that would adjudicate depth is not identified, since the layer points cluster in a handful of backbones while mean cosine, participation ratio, top eigenvalue share and depth itself are mutually collinear, and the prior record already reports the raw layer choice as statistically null once standardisation enters, at an effect size below the floor of the frame the headline lives in.

It will not answer anything about pooling, chunking or attention over residues. The deepest observable this lab has is one mean-pooled vector per protein per config; there is no layer column and no residue column in the embedding table. Residue retention for esm2_150m alone is 528,294 sequences by 625 mean residues by 640 dimensions at two bytes, which is 423 gigabytes against 568 free, 74 percent of the disk for one of eight models. That question needs a disk before it needs a design.

It will not answer anything self-supervised, and the manuscript must stop implying otherwise. There is no predictor, no exponential-moving-average target encoder, no masking, and GO labels throughout including in the mined negatives. The object is supervised metric learning with mined hard negatives, and the cross-backbone arm answers the label-free question with one target swap without substituting for a masked-prediction programme.

It will not answer the nonlinear question. The capacity argument that justifies the flatness control holds only for a linear map under a cosine loss, so a two-layer encoder cannot be slotted into this grid and would need its own controls.

It will not resolve fine-grained no-knowledge claims. Three hundred and ninety-nine proteins across three aspects and three length bands averages 44 per cell, and any stratum whose minimum detectable effect exceeds the effect under discussion reports a point estimate and no null.

It will not generalise across evaluation cuts, since it runs one cut and one bank, and freezing the information-content artifact means a new cut invalidates every fit rather than appending to it.

It will not touch five of the eight canonical models, so nothing here speaks to structure-aware pretraining or to the ESM-C family, and it will not explain the ankh_base K anomaly, since ankh_base is not on the ladder.

It will not measure the interaction between K and the encoder, deliberately, because the mining radius is frozen at 30 to buy independence between those two axes, and that independence is worth more than the interaction.

And a clean negative here will not be a verdict on sparse representations in general. It will be a verdict on a top-k selection over an affine projection of mean-pooled vectors, at the sparsity levels listed above, trained on a Lin target, scored by nearest-neighbour transfer at K equal to 3. That is the claim the thesis makes, and it is the claim this plan will settle.
