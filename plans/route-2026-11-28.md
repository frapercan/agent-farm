## What changed

The author set five transversal seeds for the whole project:

1. a complete product that explains and shows its validation, and also works as a tool for other annotation groups;
2. product documentation on PyPI;
3. the thesis project;
4. the project's semantics aligned across all parts;
5. quality of abstraction, using the best patterns.

The goal is to put the variants of our own annotation methods into production. Before that, every research method is read on the 211->227 window inside the platform: the literature ladder, the segment encoder and the annotation world model, the lexicon's segment encoding and its count map, the trees over the sources, the October reader, and a stratum mixture (routing by stratum: a weighted mix of the adopted methods per stratum, with shrinkage toward the parent stratum). Methods run through one image (`method-runtime`) with three verbs (describe, build, predict) over bundles kept in sha256 compartments, and LAFA runs the same image.

The fixed protocol does not change: instance 211 fits on 160->194, 177->205 and 194->211 and selects on 211->227 by confirmation only; instance 227 refits and reads 227->230 once, at the end, by the author's word.

The full route (43 steps, each closed by a toy check in DEV and a small review on the five seeds) lives on the desktop at `~/Thesis/escalera/roadmap/roadmap.md`. Each hand-over below comes with its own note carrying everything the laptop needs.
