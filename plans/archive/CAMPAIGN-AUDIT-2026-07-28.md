## Campaign objective

Regenerate every thesis number from the most primordial level under one governing invariant — no artifact without a registered platform operation — so that the four-pillar thesis rests on a chain where every number carries a job id and a provenance receipt.

Scientifically the run must settle whether the two hard cells (LK-BPO, PK-BPO) fall on the internal adjustment split scored with our own evaluator (D-05: nine of nine, per cell, no gate anywhere referencing an external number), or whether the campaign instead delivers the sharpest available characterisation of the separability-and-calibration frontier. CAMPAIGN §8 declares both outcomes publishable; the second is Pillar 4, not a failure.

The run is structured as Stages A–G (corpus → representation → structures → frames → candidates/features → models → measurement), then a five-rung ladder: R1 weighted-score benchmark (PLM × K × score weighting, per-cell winner, gated on recall@30 and IA-coverage), R2 the learned sparse encoder at equal input across aggregator recipes A–F (B is the load-bearing control; A vs B isolates chunking, B vs C isolates order), R3 full-vocabulary classifier + regime routing, R4 signal integration by removal per cell with intervals — which PRODUCES the signal registry as the deliverable — and R5 ensembling in three separately-measured forms. The board metric (f_micro_w, LAFA) decides only at R5 and only for the frozen champion.

Measurement discipline is the actual contribution as much as the score: four axes on every number (category × aspect = 9 reported cells; length buckets at model context limits; homology band × experimental-evidence), no pooled or aggregate number ever, ground truth as first appearance rather than pairwise difference (the retired rule leaks up to 63.7% of apparent additions on all-evidence data, ~1% on experimental), and the prior standing demoted to context because its final increment was selected on test.
